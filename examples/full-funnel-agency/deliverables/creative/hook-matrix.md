# Hook matrix — Kettleworks, flight 1

**Opened:** 2026-08-19 · **Owner:** creative-strategist · **Skill:** `hook-matrix`
**Read against:** [`weekly-reconciliation.md`](../measurement/weekly-reconciliation.md) only. No platform-reported number sets a cell's status.

10 cells populated of a possible 72. Choosing which ten is the strategic work; the
selection rationale is at the bottom.

---

## Axes

**Hooks** — the claim or tension the first two seconds carry. Each is falsifiable.

| id | Hook |
|---|---|
| H1 | Your grinder is the bottleneck, not your beans |
| H2 | The same beans, ground two ways |
| H3 | Uneven burr alignment is why your espresso pulls sour on one side |
| H4 | What an inconsistent grind costs you in wasted beans per month |
| H5 | Temperature stability, shown rather than claimed |
| H6 | Why people who do this for a living weigh everything |

**Audiences** — defined by what they already believe, not by demographics.

| id | Audience |
|---|---|
| A1 | Owns an entry-level grinder and suspects it is the bottleneck |
| A2 | Buys good beans and blames the beans |
| A3 | Has the gear, wants repeatability shot to shot |

**Formats** — a variable, not a wrapper. The same hook in two formats is two cells.

`VID` short-form video · `STA` static · `CAR` carousel · `CMP` side-by-side comparison

## Cells

### H3-A1-VID

- **Hypothesis:** A1 will act on H3 because they have already noticed the symptom (uneven extraction) and have attributed it to their technique rather than their equipment. Naming the mechanism converts a vague dissatisfaction into a diagnosable fault.
- **Primary signal:** 3-second hold rate. Shallowest causal read for a hook test — a claim test would need click-through and a full-funnel test would need three weeks.
- **Kill:** hold rate below 22% after 40,000 impressions, whichever comes first.
- **Scale:** hold rate at or above 30% sustained over 7 days.
- **Reads on:** 2026-08-28
- **Status:** live (2026-08-21) · **Brief:** [`briefs/h3-a1-vid.md`](briefs/h3-a1-vid.md)

### H1-A2-VID

- **Hypothesis:** A2 will act on H1 because their bean spend already signals they care about the outcome; the hook reassigns the blame they are currently placing on roasters.
- **Primary signal:** 3-second hold rate.
- **Kill:** below 22% after 40,000 impressions. **Scale:** at or above 30% over 7 days.
- **Reads on:** 2026-08-28 · **Status:** live (2026-08-21)

### H2-A2-CMP

- **Hypothesis:** A2 needs to see the difference rather than be told it; a side-by-side of one bean ground two ways is the shortest path from their belief to ours.
- **Primary signal:** click-through. This one is a claim test, not a hook test — the comparison *is* the claim.
- **Kill:** click-through below 0.9% after $1,200 spend. **Scale:** at or above 1.6% over 7 days.
- **Reads on:** 2026-08-31 · **Status:** live (2026-08-21)

### H5-A3-VID

- **Hypothesis:** A3 discounts claims by default and responds to demonstration; showing temperature holding across a sequence of pulls is evidence in their own vocabulary.
- **Primary signal:** 3-second hold rate.
- **Kill:** below 22% after 40,000 impressions. **Scale:** at or above 30% over 7 days.
- **Reads on:** 2026-08-28 · **Status:** live (2026-08-21)

- **Hypothesis:** Same hypothesis as H3-A1-VID, different format. Isolates whether the mechanism explanation needs motion to land.

- **Reads on:** 2026-09-04 · **Status:** planned (flight 1b)

## Why these ten

- **Three cells share A1** (H3-A1-VID, H3-A1-STA, H1-A1-STA) so format and hook can each be isolated within one audience. Comparative ranking is only legitimate within a shared audience.
- **H3 appears in two formats** because if the mechanism explanation wins in both, the *hook* is the finding and everything else can vary around it in flight 2.
- **H4 is deliberately last.** It is the closest to a cost argument, and the program does not run price-led messaging — the cell is framed on wasted beans, not on money saved, and it is the one most likely to be rewritten at brief stage.
- **What this rules out:** nothing tests packaging, provenance, or brand-story angles in flight 1. If all six hooks underperform, that absence is the next place to look, and it is an absence by choice rather than by oversight.
