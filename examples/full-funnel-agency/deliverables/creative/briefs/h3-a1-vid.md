# Brief: H3-A1-VID — "Sour on one side"

**Cell:** H3-A1-VID (hypothesis lives in [`../hook-matrix.md`](../hook-matrix.md); not restated here)
**Placement:** Meta, Reels and Stories, 9:16, 1080×1920, under 20 seconds
**Due:** 2026-08-20 — flight starts 2026-08-21
**Skill:** `creative-brief`

## The one thing

A viewer must leave believing their uneven espresso is a fixable equipment fault with a
name, not a personal skill failure.

## Must contain

- **A visible symptom in the first 2 seconds** — one shot pulling faster on one side of a
  bottomless portafilter. This is the hook; without it on frame 1 the cell is testing
  something else.
- **The word "alignment"** spoken and on screen. The hypothesis is that naming the
  mechanism is what converts dissatisfaction into a diagnosable fault.
- **A burr set shown out of true, then true.** The claim needs a physical referent.
- **Substantiation on screen for the extraction claim:** the same dose and grind setting
  before and after, stated as text. Source: internal bench test, 2026-06, 12 pulls.

## Must not contain

- **Any offer, discount, sale, coupon, or price framing.** Out of scope for the program in
  every channel. If the edit feels like it needs one to close, the close is wrong.
- **A comparison to a named competitor product.** Not prohibited by the goal, but it moves
  the test from "does the mechanism explanation land" to "do we win a comparison", and the
  cell cannot answer both.
- **Voiceover claims about health, sleep, or caffeine.**

## Shape

- **First 2 seconds:** bottomless portafilter, close, one side running visibly faster.
  No logo, no title card, no music intro. The symptom is the hook.
- **Middle:** hand lifts the hopper; burr set shown out of true against a straightedge;
  alignment corrected; same dose re-pulled and running even. Text on screen names the
  fault once.
- **Close:** "Check yours" and the product's alignment feature, stated the way the landing
  page states it. No urgency device.

## Copy

- **Primary (Meta, ≤125 characters before truncation):**
  "One side runs fast, the other runs slow. That is usually burr alignment, not your
  technique." — 94 characters.
- **Headline (≤40 characters):** "Sour on one side? Check alignment" — 33 characters.
- **Alternates** — vary only the naming of the fault, nothing else:
  1. "That uneven pull has a name: burr alignment." — 44 characters.
  2. "Not your technique. Your burrs are out of true." — 47 characters.
