## Decision: no spend moves this week

Written down deliberately. The skill's rule is that a review whose only possible output is
a change will invent one, so here is the reasoning for standing still.

The reallocation rule requires all three to hold. Check them against the only row that
could receive money — Meta retargeting, the sole row under target:

1. **Receiving row's constraint is `budget`** — holds. Retargeting is capped and hungry.
2. **At or under target for two consecutive 28-day windows** — **fails.** This is the
   *first* window with reconciled numbers behind it. The prior window's figures came from
   platform reports that overstated conversions by 16.0%, so there is one trustworthy
   window, not two. Moving on one window is moving on noise wearing a table's clothes.
3. **Giving row is `auction`/`creative`, or over target twice** — holds for Meta
   prospecting (`creative`, and over target).

Two of three. **No move.**

Independently, the skill forbids citing an unreconciled row in a spend decision, which
rules out Google Shopping/PMax (+11.4%) and Meta prospecting (+83.0%) as sources or
destinations regardless of the above.

**What would have happened if the rule had held:** the cap allows at most 20% of the giving
row's budget in one week — **$6,960** of Meta prospecting's $34,800. Not a dollar more,
because a larger move resets learning on both sides and costs two weeks to find out.

**Re-check date:** 2026-08-31, when the second reconciled window closes.
