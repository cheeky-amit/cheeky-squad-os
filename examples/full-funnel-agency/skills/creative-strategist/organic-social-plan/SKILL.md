# organic-social-plan

Source: https://github.com/msitarzewski/agency-agents/blob/main/marketing/marketing-instagram-curator.md

Adapted from that agent's aesthetic framework, content mix, and multi-format planning.
Two things were kept and one core assumption was inverted.

**Kept:** the format-mix discipline and the grid-level coherence check. Both are real
and both are routinely skipped.

**Inverted:** the source plans organic as its own creative universe, with its own
aesthetic development and its own testing. In a squad where paid is already testing
concepts at volume against real money, that is a second, slower, less-instrumented test
of the same questions. Here, **organic inherits from paid.** A concept earns an organic
slot by having survived a hook-matrix cell, or by belonging to one of two exception
categories below. This is the "don't reinvent the wheel" rule applied inside the squad,
not just outside it.

## What may occupy a slot

1. **Validated concepts** — a hook-matrix cell marked `scaled`, adapted to the platform's
   native shape. Cite the cell id.
2. **Community material** — customer-made content, questions actually asked, replies.
   No paid validation needed; this is not a test.
3. **Category education** — the durable explainer content that answers a question the
   organic search briefs are already targeting. Coordinate with the lifecycle-organic
   seat so a video and a page are not written independently about the same query.

Anything that fits none of these three is a concept looking for a budget. Send it to the
hook matrix.

## The 30-day mix

Plan slots, not posts. A slot has a date, a format, a source category, and an owner.

- **Roughly half** validated concepts — the compounding half. This is where the grid
  earns its aesthetic coherence, because scaled concepts already share a look.
- **Roughly a third** community material — the half-life is short, so plan the *sourcing
  cadence*, not the individual items.
- **The remainder** category education — the slowest-moving, longest-lived, and the only
  category worth a production budget on its own.

Ratios are a starting shape, not a rule to defend. What is a rule: **every slot names
its source category**, and every validated slot names its cell id.

## Coherence check

Before the month ships, view the planned slots as a set:

- Do three consecutive slots make the same claim in three formats? That is repetition,
  not a campaign — vary the claim.
- Does the set read as one brand at a glance — colour, framing, type, pacing?
- Is any single product carrying more than half the slots?
- Is there a week with nothing timely in it, where a community slot could absorb whatever
  actually happens?

## Rules

1. **Never post a concept paid killed.** If it failed against money, organic reach will
   not rescue it — it will just be seen by fewer people.
2. **No promotional, discount, sale, or price-led posts.** Out of scope for this program
   in every channel, organic included.
3. **Organic performance does not overrule paid results.** A post that outperforms
   organically is a signal to send the concept *back* to the hook matrix as a new cell,
   not to declare it a winner.
4. **Every slot has one owner and a production lead time** long enough for the brief and
   the review to happen.

## Output

Write to `deliverables/creative/organic-plan-<YYYY-MM>.md`: the slot table (date, format,
source category, cell id where applicable, owner, lead time), the coherence check
answers, and a one-line note on anything deliberately left open for timely material.

## Scope boundary

This skill plans distribution of concepts that already exist. It does not create the
hypothesis ([`hook-matrix`](../hook-matrix/SKILL.md)) or specify production
([`creative-brief`](../creative-brief/SKILL.md)) — an organic slot that needs production
raises a normal brief against its cell id.

## Done when

- Every slot names a source category, and every validated slot names its cell id.
- The coherence check has been run and its answers are written down.
- No slot repeats a killed concept.
- Every slot has an owner and a lead time.
